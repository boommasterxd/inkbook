# Light Toolbar Active-Tool Contrast

The project owner explicitly accepted the current active-pen contrast in Obsidian's default light theme. This decision covers the glyph and the active indicator outline at rest.

The glyph measures 2.73:1 against its indicator fill. The outline measures 1.77:1 against the toolbar. Both fall below the 3:1 non-text contrast target, so the active tool may be difficult to distinguish. The decision records that accessibility gap without changing the target or extending the exception to other states, controls, or themes.

The [accessibility acceptance contract](../quality-and-testing.md#accessibility-acceptance) applies to the remaining toolbar states and controls.
