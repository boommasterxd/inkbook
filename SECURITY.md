# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to `kontakt@boomapps.de`. Do not open a public issue or include secrets, private Vault data, or unnecessary personal data.

Include the affected version, platform, Obsidian version, reproducible steps, impact, and the smallest safe proof needed to validate the report. You should receive an acknowledgement within seven calendar days. No remediation deadline or reward is guaranteed.

Only the latest development revision and later supported releases are in scope. Third-party services, Obsidian itself, social engineering, denial-of-service traffic against GitHub, and findings that require unsafe handling of another person's Vault are out of scope.

This policy is not a promise that the software has no vulnerabilities.
